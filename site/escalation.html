Method

Midpoint of construction = construction start plus half the duration. Escalation months = months from the pricing date to the midpoint. Escalation factor = (1 + annual rate) ^ (months ÷ 12).

Trailing rate = compound annual change between the average index of the latest 12 months and the average of the same 12 months 1, 3 or 5 years earlier. A trailing rate describes the past. It is not a forecast; pick the rate you believe for the project period, and use the custom rate to enter a published forecast.

Selling-price vs cost indexes. BLS building-type and trade-contractor PPIs measure what contractors charge, like the Turner, RLB and Mortenson indexes. Material PPIs measure input costs only. Published guidance, including Ed Zarenski's at edzarenski.com, warns against escalating whole buildings with material cost indexes.
