Method and source

Source. . Base year 2024 = 100. Values for and later are Construction Analytics forecasts. Turner, Rider Levett Bucknall, Mortenson, ENR, RS Means, IHS, FHWA, Bureau of Reclamation, Census, S&P/Case-Shiller and BLS PPI series are as compiled by Construction Analytics.

Monthly values. Each annual index is an annual average, so it is placed at mid-year (July) and months between are interpolated geometrically. Dates past July of the last table year are extended at that index's final annual rate and flagged.

Midpoint. Construction start plus half the duration. Escalation = index at midpoint ÷ index at pricing date − 1.
