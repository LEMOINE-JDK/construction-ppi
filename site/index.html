Method and sources

Cost vs selling price. Material inputs are cost indexes: what contractors pay for materials, excluding labor, overhead and margin. Building types and trade contractors are selling-price indexes: what contractors charge for finished work. Use the selling-price series, not the material series, to escalate a whole-building budget. When inputs rise faster than selling prices, contractor margins are being squeezed.

Flags. No new price marks a series whose index has been identical for 3 or more months, which usually means BLS carried a value forward rather than prices holding flat. Note marks a series with a data caveat; hover for details.

Quarterly view. BLS revises building-type and trade indexes each January, April, July and October, so quarter-to-quarter comparisons are more reliable than month-to-month.
