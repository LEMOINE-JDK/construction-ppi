Method and sources

Flags. No new price marks a series whose index has been identical for 3 or more months, which usually means BLS carried a value forward rather than prices holding flat. Note marks a series with a data caveat; hover for details.

Material inputs measure what contractors pay. Building types and trade contractors measure what contractors charge for the finished work. When inputs rise faster than building prices, contractor margins are being squeezed.
