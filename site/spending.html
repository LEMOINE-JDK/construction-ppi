Method and sources

Spending is the value of construction put in place each month, at a seasonally adjusted annual rate (SAAR). Year to date sums actual monthly dollars, not seasonally adjusted, as Census reports it.

Constant dollars restate each month's spending in the latest month's prices using the BLS selling-price index for new nonresidential buildings (PPI WPU801). Real change shows whether more building is happening, or the same work is costing more. It is applied to building markets; for Power and total construction it is only an approximation.

Nonresidential Buildings is the sum of the building markets listed under it. Office includes data centers. Census publishes data centers separately only in its detailed spreadsheets, not in its data feed.

Census revises the prior two months with each release and benchmarks the full series each July, so recent months change.
