Method and sources

Jobs are payroll employees in thousands, seasonally adjusted unless noted. Changes for job counts are shown in thousands month over month and in percent year over year; rates change in percentage points.

Productivity: Productivity here is output per worker on nonresidential building work. It moves with both efficiency and the mix of work, so read it as a trend, not a precise measure.

BLS revises the last two months of national jobs data each release and benchmarks annually. Metro and state data come from a smaller survey and are noisier.
